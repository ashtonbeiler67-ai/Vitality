Red Lentil Coconut Dal

A home-cooked favorite. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 250 g red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 x 400 ml tin full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 750 ml vegetable stock
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
