Heirloom Caprese Salad

A home-cooked favorite. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz BelGioioso fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • 1 lb heirloom tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • A handful of fresh basil leaves
  • Flaky salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
