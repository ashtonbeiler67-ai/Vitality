Turkey Breakfast Burritos

A home-cooked favorite. 4 burritos.

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 4 (10-inch) flour tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese
  • 1/2 lb 93% lean ground turkey
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • Cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
