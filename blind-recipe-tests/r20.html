Cheesy Smash Burgers

A home-cooked favorite. 4 burgers.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb 90% lean ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 oz cream cheese (Philadelphia), softened
  • Lettuce and tomato, for serving

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
