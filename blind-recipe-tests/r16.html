Penne with Basil, Pecorino and Pine Nuts

A home-cooked favorite. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 400g penne
  • 60 ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 50 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
  • 30 g pine nuts, toasted
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 60 g fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
