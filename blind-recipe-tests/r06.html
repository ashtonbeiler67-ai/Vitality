Salmon Quinoa Power Bowls

A home-cooked favorite. 2 bowls.

Ingredients

  • 2 (6 oz) salmon fillets, skin removed
  • 3/4 cup dry quinoa
  • 1 ripe avocado, sliced
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
