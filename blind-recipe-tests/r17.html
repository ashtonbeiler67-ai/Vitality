Teriyaki Chicken Rice Plates

A home-cooked favorite. 2 plates.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz cooked chicken breast, sliced
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 2 cups steamed broccoli
  • 2 tbsp teriyaki sauce

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
