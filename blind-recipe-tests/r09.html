Greek Sheet Pan Chicken and Potatoes

A home-cooked favorite. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 kg baby potatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/2 cup pitted black olives

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
