Shrimp Fried Rice

A home-cooked favorite. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cooked day-old white rice
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 8 oz peeled raw shrimp

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
