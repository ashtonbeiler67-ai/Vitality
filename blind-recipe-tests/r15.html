Chicken Oaxaca Quesadillas

A home-cooked favorite. Serves 2.

Ingredients

  • 4 (8-inch) flour tortillas
  • 6 oz Oaxaca cheese, shredded
  • 1/4 cup crumbled cotija
  • 1 cup cooked shredded chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp butter, for the pan

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
