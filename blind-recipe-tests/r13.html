Fluffy British Pancakes

A home-cooked favorite. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 300 ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 30 g unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • Butter, for the pan

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
