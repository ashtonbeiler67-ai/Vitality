Lean Turkey Chili

A home-cooked favorite. 8 servings.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs 93% lean ground turkey
  • 2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 (28-oz.) can diced tomatoes
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
