Crispy Tofu Broccoli Stir-Fry

A home-cooked favorite. Serves 3.

Ingredients

  • 1 14-ounce package firm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup jasmine rice, uncooked
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, for the pan

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
