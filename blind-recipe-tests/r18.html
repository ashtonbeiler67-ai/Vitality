Strawberry Protein Smoothie

A home-cooked favorite. 1 smoothie.

Ingredients

  • 1 scoop (30 g) vanilla whey protein
  • 1 cup frozen strawberries
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1/2 frozen banana

Instructions

  1. Prep all the ingredients.
  2. Cook everything as described, stirring often.
  3. Taste, adjust seasoning and serve.
